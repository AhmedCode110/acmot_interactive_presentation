Research problem

Why Is Aerial Multi-Object Tracking Hard?

Look for tiny objects, crowding and large scale changes.

Tiny targets

Cars and people may occupy only a few pixels.

Dense scenes

Nearby objects make matching and identity preservation harder.

Changing image quality

Blur, darkness and edges vary from frame to frame.

Key idea: scene difficulty is not constant, so one detector setting may not always be efficient.

Point at the image while explaining each difficulty. The image is not decoration; it shows the reason for adaptation.

Scene Complexity Index

Five Signals Describe Scene Difficulty

Each cue answers a simple question about this frame.

1 · Crowd

How many tracked objects were present?

2 · Tiny objects

What fraction of boxes are smaller than 32 × 32?

3 · Edge complexity

How visually busy is the frame?

4 · Low light

Is the grayscale mean below the night threshold?

5 · Blur

Is Laplacian variance low?

Worked example

SCI: One Simple Numerical Example

Crowd

15 / 30 = 0.50

Tiny fraction

6 / 15 = 0.40

Edge norm

0.07 / 0.14 = 0.50
SCI = w₁·crowd + w₂·tiny + w₃·edge + w₄·night + w₅·blur
Then smooth it: use a rolling window of 7 SCI readings and analyze every 10 frames, so the controller does not jump on every small change.

Scientific claim

What Can We Defend Now?

Supported

  • scene-aware detector control is practical
  • the idea works in the lightweight YOLOv8n + ByteTrack track
  • it transfers to a very different YOLOX-X + U2MOT framework
  • it can reduce IDS and FP while approximately preserving MOTA / IDF1
  • adaptive resolution can reduce matched-runtime compute

Not supported

  • AC-MOT improves every tracker
  • AC-MOT always improves every metric
  • the final U2MOT MOTA is higher
  • historical custom results are official leaderboard results
  • Q1 acceptance is guaranteed

Honest limitations

What Still Limits the Current Study?

Final MOTA

U2MOT test-dev MOTA fell by 0.133 points.

False negatives

FN increased by 1,560 on final test-dev.

Hard-set dominance

66.62% of test-dev frames used the Hard regime.

Pipeline dependence

The best detector actions can differ by model.

Hand-designed cues

SCI is not yet a learned difficulty estimator.

Official lightweight rerun

The original track still needs a clean official-aligned rerun for the strongest paper story.

Ablation depth

Full official confidence-only / NMS-only / resolution-only ablation would strengthen causality.

Statistics

Per-sequence paired analysis should be added to the latest official-aligned track.
